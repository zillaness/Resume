Projects

Lattice footwear

A computational design arc from early Grasshopper definitions to current lattice engines: a running sole on a fractal diamond truss, a slide with a BCC core and Voronoi net skin, a flip-flop on a graded sheet gyroid. Current and ongoing; renders for now, functional prototype to come.

FumeX: solder fume extractor

Three stages of filtration on a radial fan: pre-filter, H13 HEPA, activated carbon, so students can solder without PPE-grade equipment. Built for Rambots 2204; published as a build video and downloadable model.

Flip It: motorized monitor mount

Sandwiches between any monitor and stand on standard VESA holes and rotates the screen 90° in about ten seconds from a desk-mounted toggle. A cam shifts the monitor forward as it turns, so adjacent bezels clear.

Gravity car

Welded steel frame with coilover suspension and printed aerodynamic wheel pods. Won longest average distance; it ran straight on every run. I'm most proud though of my repair kit which I shared with competitors allowing them to compete at their very best.

Variably compliant gripper

A robot gripper that squeezes less at the bottom and more at the top, so one mechanism holds both a cone and a cube. I suggested the concept and gave feedback over multiple cycles of design reviews; but ultimately the design and award belongs to the students of FRC Team 2204 Rambots.

Crab gauge

A California legal-size gauge shaped like a Dungeness crab. Dungeness measures between the front claws, rock crab between the back legs. Two species, one tool. Kaitlyn Bui, a San Francisco fishing creator, used my design in one of her own videos and even featured it in the trailer for a CAAM documentary.

Pacific Pals headlamp

Concept and render for a headlamp in a hiking accessory line group project. The charge indicator doubles as a red night light, to preserve night vision.

Future Nintendo console concept

A dockable console whose screen slides out of the handheld shell into a VR headset, the detached controllers coming along. One set of hardware, three modes: docked, handheld, headset.

Adhesive pull test

Started as a claw machine build from scratch. A printer is already an XYZ motion system, so the plan became repurposing one; the design that came out of it is a kit that converts a printer into a working claw machine and is fully reversible.
